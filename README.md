**1. sss-ssg.live/**
Website: [sss-ssg.live](https://sss-ssg.live)

**sss-ssg.live** is a real-time solar activity and imaging platform.  
It collects, processes, and displays data from multiple space-based solar observatories and ground-based monitoring stations to provide up-to-date insight into solar flares, active regions, coronal mass ejections, sunspots, and more.

## 🚀 Projects & Platforms

- 📱 **[FlareAlert](https://flarealert.live)** – Real-time solar flare tracking platform and Android app with instant notifications, including flare class, active region, location, and live image links. Designed for speed and especially useful for solar observers and solar imagers.

- 🌐 **[sss-ssg.live](https://sss-ssg.live)** – Solar & astro imaging platform providing real-time solar data, imagery, and event visualization from space-based missions and ground-based observatories.

- 🔭 **[sss-ssg.ch](https://sss-ssg.ch)** – Solar and astro imaging documentation, observational setups, and equipment overview.

---

## 🛰️ Space-Based Solar Missions

- ☀️ **[SOHO](https://sss-ssg.live/soho)** – Continuous observations of the Sun’s atmosphere and solar wind from the L1 point, including LASCO coronagraph imagery.  
  - 🔭 LASCO: **[SOHO LASCO](https://sss-ssg.live/soho/lasco)**

- 🌞 **[SDO – AIA 304 Å](https://sss-ssg.live/sdo)** – Real-time Solar Dynamics Observatory imagery highlighting the chromosphere, prominences, filaments, and active regions.

- 📡 **[GOES-R SUVI](https://sss-ssg.live/goes-r/suvi304-1/)** – Real-time extreme ultraviolet (EUV) solar imagery used to monitor solar flares, coronal structures, and space weather activity.

---

## 🔭 Ground-Based Stations & Networks

- 🌍 **[GONG H-Alpha](https://sss-ssg.live/gong/)** – Global ground-based H-alpha network providing continuous monitoring of prominences, filaments, sunspots, and chromospheric activity.

- 🧪 **LMSAL (Lockheed Martin Solar & Astrophysics Laboratory)** – Solar physics research laboratory that designs and operates space-based instruments and provides scientific data for solar activity and solar event tracking.

  **Related Project – FlareAlert (FAAPP):**  
  Website: **[https://flarealert.live](https://flarealert.live)**  
  Features: **[https://flarealert.live/features/](https://flarealert.live/features/)**

  📱 Platform: **Android (notifications-based)**

- 🏔️ **[Kanzelhöhe Solar Observatory – Hα (2026)](https://sss-ssg.live/kso/)** – Ground-based H-alpha prominence imagery capturing solar prominences and chromospheric dynamics.

## Swiss Solar Observation Society 

- 🔭 **[80mm f/7 ED APO Refractor](https://sss-ssg.ch/TS80mmAPO.html)** – High-quality refractor used for solar and astrophotography.

- ☀️ **[40mm LS40THa/B500](https://sss-ssg.ch/LS40THaB500.html)** – Dedicated H-alpha solar telescope for chromospheric observation.

- 🔥 **[LS40THa + Etalon + DayStar Quark](https://sss-ssg.ch/lunt-solar-system.html)** – Advanced H-alpha solar imaging setup for high-resolution solar surface and prominence detail.
